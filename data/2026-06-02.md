We present LiftNav, a hybrid navigation framework built on GSFusion's TSDF+GS dual map, augmented with a real-time pipeline of YOLO-based detection, TSDF-based 3D lifting, and B-spline trajectory optimization. This design enables flexible semantic navigation without dense 3D embeddings. We further introduce a hinge-loss-based collision penalty that improves trajectory smoothness and safety. We evaluate our approach in a simulation using the Replica dataset. Compared against a state-of-the-art radiance field baseline we show a 100% feasibility rate and shorter trajectories.
